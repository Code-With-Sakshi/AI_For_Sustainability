SAATHIAI SUSTAINABILITY KNOWLEDGE BASE
========================================

Purpose:
This knowledge base provides concise sustainability information for the SaathiAI prototype.
The RAG layer retrieves relevant sections before generating user guidance.

IMPORTANT:
This is a prototype knowledge base. For production deployment, information should be expanded
and verified using authoritative government, UN, municipal, and other trusted sources.


[ENERGY]

Air conditioners can consume significant electricity. Using appropriate temperature settings,
timers, and sleep modes can help avoid unnecessary energy consumption.

Keeping doors and windows closed while air conditioning is running can reduce the loss of
cooled air.

Regular cleaning and maintenance of air-conditioner filters can help maintain efficient operation.

Avoid operating air conditioning in an empty room when it is not necessary.

Energy-saving advice should consider the user's climate, appliance condition, household needs,
and applicable local guidance.


[WATER]

Leaking taps and pipes can cause unnecessary water loss. Leaks should be repaired promptly.

Turning off taps when water is not needed helps avoid unnecessary water consumption.

Water use can often be reduced by avoiding unnecessary running water during everyday activities.

Suitable household water may sometimes be reused for activities such as gardening, depending
on its cleanliness, source, and safety.

Water-saving recommendations should not encourage reuse when the water may create a health
or sanitation risk.


[WASTE]

Waste should be handled according to its material type and local waste-management rules.

Reusable items should be considered for reuse or repair before disposal when practical.

Recyclable materials should be separated where local collection systems support recycling.

Avoid mixing hazardous or special waste with ordinary household waste.


[ELECTRONIC WASTE]

Electronic waste can include old chargers, phones, batteries, cables, computers, and other
electronic equipment.

Electronic waste should not automatically be mixed with ordinary household waste.

E-waste should be taken to an appropriate collection, recycling, or authorized disposal channel
where available.

Batteries may require separate handling depending on local rules and battery type.


[SUSTAINABLE CONSUMPTION]

Before buying a new product, consider whether an existing product can be repaired, reused,
maintained, borrowed, or shared.

Choosing durable products and avoiding unnecessary purchases can help reduce resource use.

Users should consider product lifespan, repairability, reuse, and appropriate end-of-life
management when making purchasing decisions.


[GENERAL SUSTAINABILITY]

Sustainability recommendations should be practical, understandable, and appropriate to the
user's situation.

SaathiAI should ask only the questions necessary to provide useful guidance.

The system should avoid presenting uncertain information as fact.

When a question requires location-specific regulations, disposal facilities, health guidance,
or other high-impact information, SaathiAI should direct the user to appropriate official or
local sources.


[RESPONSIBLE AI]

SaathiAI should minimize unnecessary collection or storage of personal information.

Users should be informed when they are interacting with AI.

Recommendations should be grounded in reliable information whenever possible.

The system should avoid assumptions about a user's income, education, language, household,
or access to resources.

The user remains in control of the final decision.
